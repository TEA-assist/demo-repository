01 — TEA — Overview

TEA is an AI product finder for fashion retail — shoppers describe what they want in natural language (English or Arabic) and the system returns ranked product cards with images, prices, and store identity. Sellers manage their catalog and have AI-generated bilingual descriptions; admins curate the catalog, models, prompts, and search quality.

Problem & value

  • Catalogs are multilingual, attribute-rich, and large (7,000+ products, 67k+ images in the demo). Keyword search fails on synonyms, paraphrases, and typos.
  • Writing trilingual descriptions at scale is slow and inconsistent.
  • TEA solves both: hybrid search (dense + BM25 sparse vectors fused with RRF, plus pg_trgm trigram similarity for seller lists) and LLM-generated descriptions (en/ar) with prompt-template control.

Roadmap snapshot

  • Search ranking tuning via metrics (MRR@10 / Recall@10) after full reindex.
  • Qdrant + MinIO scheduled backups (see product-graph-api dev-tools/), Loki retention, ENGINE_API_KEY wiring.
  • Mobile store build pipeline (production keystore, versioning) — currently documented as manual in demo docs.
