00 — TEA — Executive Summary

Live on asktea.ai 7,682 products EN + AR Web + Mobile

The one-liner

TEA is an AI product finder for fashion retail. Shoppers describe what they want in plain words — in English or Arabic, typos included — and TEA returns ranked product cards with images, prices and store identity. No keyword gymnastics, no endless filters.

Why it matters (business value)

ProblemTEA answerWho benefits
Keyword search fails on synonyms, paraphrases, typos across multilingual catalogsHybrid AI search: dense vectors + BM25 sparse fused with RRF + trigram fuzzy rankingShoppers convert faster; sellers get found
Writing bilingual descriptions for 7,000+ products is slow and inconsistentOne-click LLM descriptions (en/ar) with 6 controllable prompt templates + history/restoreSellers list faster; catalog quality rises
Search quality is usually a black boxBuilt-in evaluation lab: 166-query golden set, MRR@10 / Recall@10 overall + EN/ARStakeholders see measurable quality

What is live today

  • Shopper web (app.asktea.ai): anonymous AI chat (no account), random-products slider, login via OTP/Google, favorites.
  • Dashboard (asktea.ai): admin (8 tiles) + seller (stores, products, 6-step wizard, AI descriptions).
  • Mobile: React Native 0.86 shopper app (feed + chat drawer + product modal + favorites), flavor APKs.
  • Platform: FastAPI + AI Engine (Qwen3-Embedding-4B, DeepSeek V4 Flash) + Qdrant + Postgres + MinIO + Swarm/Caddy.
