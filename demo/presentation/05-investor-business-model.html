05 — Investor Presentation: Business Model, Revenue Strategy & Financial Scenarios

Investor Presentation Live on asktea.ai EN + AR No inventory

AI-powered fashion discovery & retail technology platform. This page presents the finalized business model: consumer discovery + retailer AI assistant + partnerships + premium services, with hypothetical financial scenarios. All figures in USD and hypothetical unless stated as live metrics in the appendix.

1. Business Overview

Our company is developing an AI-powered fashion discovery platform that helps customers find products that match their personal preferences, style, budget, and shopping needs.

Instead of navigating complicated filters across multiple online stores, customers can describe what they want in natural language and receive relevant product recommendations from participating fashion brands and retailers.

For example, a customer might ask:

I'm looking for an elegant, modest summer dress for a wedding, preferably in beige and under $150.

The platform helps the customer discover suitable products, compare available options, and visit the retailer's website to complete the purchase.

Our business has two primary objectives:

  1. Help customers discover and purchase fashion products more easily.
  2. Help fashion retailers attract customers and improve product discovery within their own online stores.

We will pursue these objectives through two complementary products: a consumer-facing fashion discovery platform and an AI shopping assistant that retailers can integrate into their own websites.

Our revenue strategy combines affiliate commissions, retailer subscriptions, commercial partnerships, and additional services that can be introduced as the business grows.

The company will pursue four complementary revenue streams.

The company does not need to manufacture products, own fashion inventory, or operate its own delivery network. Retailers remain responsible for selling and fulfilling their products.

This allows the company to focus on product discovery, customer acquisition, and the technology that connects shoppers with relevant products.

3. Revenue Model 1: Consumer Fashion Discovery Platform

Overview

The company operates a consumer-facing platform where shoppers can discover fashion products from multiple brands and retailers through conversational AI.

Customers can search for products based on their preferences, including style, occasion, color, material, size, budget, and other relevant characteristics.

The platform presents suitable products with images, product information, retailer details, prices where available, and links to purchase.

The objective is to make the platform a destination for customers who know what they want to wear but need help finding the right products.

How the company earns money

The primary revenue source is affiliate commissions.

When a customer discovers a product through our platform, they can follow a tracked link to the retailer's website. If they subsequently complete a qualifying purchase, our company receives a commission according to the applicable commercial agreement.

For example:

The commission rate is hypothetical and does not represent a confirmed agreement with any retailer.

The company earns money from the referral rather than from the full product price. The retailer receives the product sale revenue and remains responsible for the order, delivery, and returns.

Advantages of this model

  • No need to purchase or hold inventory.
  • No need to manage product delivery.
  • Revenue can grow with the number of qualifying purchases.
  • The platform can work with multiple fashion brands and retailers.
  • Customers can use the service without paying a subscription.
  • The business can expand into additional product categories and geographic markets.

Commercial considerations

Affiliate revenue depends on customer traffic, product relevance, click-through rates, purchase conversion, average order value, and commission agreements.

Not every retailer offers an affiliate program, and not every purchase generates a commission.

The company will therefore need to establish relationships with retailers, obtain appropriate tracking arrangements, and continuously improve the shopping experience.

4. Revenue Model 2: AI Shopping Assistant for Retailers

Overview

In addition to its consumer platform, the company will offer its AI shopping technology directly to online fashion retailers.

Retailers can integrate the shopping assistant into their own websites, allowing their customers to search for products conversationally instead of relying exclusively on traditional filters and keyword searches.

For example, a customer visiting a retailer's website could ask:

I need a black dress for a formal event, with long sleeves, made from lightweight material, and priced below $200.

The assistant would help the customer find suitable products from that retailer's own catalog.

Unlike the consumer platform, which helps customers discover products across multiple stores, this service focuses on improving product discovery within an individual retailer's store.

Product delivery options

A. Website widget. Retailers add an AI shopping assistant to their existing websites without needing to build an entirely new shopping experience.

B. E-commerce plugin. The company provides integrations for supported e-commerce platforms, making the service easier for retailers to install and manage.

C. White-label solution. Retailers receive a shopping assistant customized with their own branding, logo, and visual identity. The customer interacts with the retailer's branded experience while the company provides the underlying service.

Revenue opportunities

Recommended initial pricing approach

The company should test a straightforward subscription model with a defined usage allowance.

For initial financial planning, we can use the following hypothetical prices:

  • Basic plan: $200 per month.
  • Standard plan: $400 per month.
  • Advanced plan: $600 per month.
These figures are examples for evaluating the business model, not finalized prices or verified market rates. Actual pricing should depend on customer interviews, service costs, catalog size, usage, and the value retailers receive.

Advantages of this model

  • Generates recurring revenue.
  • Does not require a large consumer audience before approaching retailers.
  • Creates direct commercial relationships with businesses.
  • Can provide a more predictable revenue base than affiliate commissions alone.
  • Allows the company to serve multiple retailers through a common service offering.

The key commercial challenge is demonstrating that the assistant provides enough value for retailers to pay for it. The company should validate this through pilot customers and measurable outcomes such as product engagement, successful searches, and conversion improvements.

5. Revenue Model 3: Retailer Partnerships and Product Distribution

Overview

The company will establish partnerships with fashion brands and retailers to make their products discoverable through its consumer platform.

Retailers can provide product information through agreed integrations and participate in the platform's product discovery experience.

The company then helps customers find relevant products and directs interested shoppers to the participating retailers.

This creates a commercial relationship in which the company acts as an additional customer acquisition channel.

The model is inspired by established fashion discovery businesses that combine consumer shopping experiences with retailer partnerships and affiliate relationships.

5.1 Affiliate networks and platforms

An important part of this strategy is working with affiliate networks. Affiliate networks connect retailers with publishers and other partners that promote products and generate customer referrals. They provide infrastructure for tracking qualifying purchases, attributing sales, reporting results, and managing commissions. Our company can participate as a publisher or affiliate partner, subject to the requirements of each network and retailer.

5.2 How the affiliate process works

  1. Step 1: Register with a suitable network. The company applies to an affiliate network and provides information about its business, website, and promotional activities.
  2. Step 2: Apply to participating retailers. The company identifies relevant fashion brands and applies to their affiliate programs, subject to their requirements.
  3. Step 3: Obtain approved tracking links. For participating products, the company uses the retailer's or network's approved affiliate tracking mechanisms.
  4. Step 4: Recommend products to customers. The AI platform recommends suitable products and directs interested customers to the retailers through those links.
  5. Step 5: Earn qualifying commissions. When purchases meet the retailer's attribution and eligibility requirements, commissions are recorded and eventually paid according to the applicable terms.

A standard UTM parameter can help with marketing analytics, but it does not by itself establish affiliate attribution or guarantee payment.

5.3 Direct retailer partnerships

Some retailers may not participate in the networks available to our company. In these cases, we can approach retailers directly and negotiate commercial agreements.

Potential arrangements include:

  • Commission on qualifying purchases.
  • Fixed fees for qualified referrals.
  • Performance-based agreements.
  • Other mutually agreed commercial arrangements.

Direct agreements would need to define how referrals are tracked, how sales are attributed, and when payments are due.

Advantages of the partnership model

  • Expands the product selection available to customers.
  • Creates additional opportunities for affiliate revenue.
  • Establishes direct relationships with brands and retailers.
  • Allows the company to work with retailers outside the available affiliate networks.
  • Creates opportunities for retailers to use the company's separate B2B shopping assistant.

The long-term objective is to build a network of participating retailers that makes the consumer platform increasingly useful while providing retailers with an additional source of customer acquisition.

6. Revenue Model 4: Premium Retail Services

Overview

Once the platform has established a meaningful customer base and sufficient commercial data, the company can introduce additional services for retailers and fashion brands. These services would complement the existing affiliate and subscription models.

A. Sponsored product placements

Retailers may pay for additional visibility within relevant product recommendations. For example, a participating brand might pay to promote a collection to customers searching for summer clothing. Sponsored placements should be clearly disclosed and remain relevant to the customer's request.

B. Premium retailer analytics

Retailers could pay for advanced reporting on product discovery and customer engagement. Potential reports include:

  • Popular search categories.
  • Frequently requested styles, colors, and price ranges.
  • Products receiving the most engagement.
  • Searches that fail to produce suitable results.
  • Product categories with high interest but low conversion.

These insights could help retailers improve their product selection and merchandising decisions. The company would need appropriate permissions and privacy safeguards before using customer activity for analytics or sharing insights with retailers.

C. Enterprise services

Larger retailers may require customized integrations, additional support, specific commercial terms, or more extensive reporting. These services could be sold through individual enterprise agreements.

Strategic role

These additional revenue streams can increase revenue per retailer and diversify the company's income. However, they should be introduced only after the core business demonstrates sufficient demand. They are opportunities for expansion, not guaranteed sources of future revenue.

8. Competitive Positioning and Strategic Advantages

The company is positioned at the intersection of consumer fashion discovery and retail technology. Its business strategy is not limited to providing a better search interface. It aims to help retailers acquire customers while offering them a service that can improve the shopping experience within their own stores.

Advantage 1: Two complementary business channels

The consumer platform creates opportunities to earn commissions from referred purchases. The retailer service generates potential recurring revenue through subscriptions and commercial agreements. This reduces dependence on a single revenue source.

Advantage 2: No inventory ownership requirement

The company does not need to purchase products, operate warehouses, or manage delivery. Retailers remain responsible for product sales and fulfillment. This allows the business to focus its resources on customer acquisition, product discovery, and retailer relationships.

Advantage 3: A growing retailer network

As more retailers participate, the consumer platform can offer a wider selection of products. A larger product selection can make the platform more useful to shoppers, potentially attracting more customers and creating further opportunities for retailer partnerships. This growth loop depends on maintaining product quality, accurate availability, relevant recommendations, and customer trust.

Advantage 4: Recurring B2B revenue

Retailer subscriptions can provide a more predictable revenue stream than affiliate commissions alone. Once the service demonstrates value, the company can expand through additional retailer subscriptions and enterprise agreements.

Advantage 5: Multiple expansion opportunities

The company can begin with product discovery and retailer subscriptions, then introduce premium analytics, sponsored placements, and additional services as demand develops. This provides flexibility without requiring the company to commit to every potential revenue stream from the beginning.

9. Business Development Strategy

The company should pursue its commercial opportunities in stages, prioritizing revenue validation before expanding into additional services.

Phase 1: Validate the consumer platform

The immediate objective is to establish that customers can discover suitable products and that the platform can generate meaningful retailer referrals. Priorities include:

  • Establishing initial retailer partnerships.
  • Exploring relevant affiliate programs.
  • Measuring customer engagement and product clicks.
  • Testing whether recommendations lead to qualifying purchases.
  • Improving product catalog coverage and search relevance.

The company should not assume that affiliate revenue will be significant until it has evidence of actual customer traffic and purchase conversion.

Phase 2: Introduce the retailer subscription service

The next objective is to validate whether retailers will pay for an AI shopping assistant. The company should approach a small number of potential pilot customers and offer a clearly defined service. The initial commercial focus should be on:

  • Demonstrating the value of conversational product discovery.
  • Measuring customer engagement and conversion where possible.
  • Establishing a sustainable subscription price.
  • Understanding retailers' willingness to pay.
  • Identifying the costs associated with serving each retailer.

Phase 3: Expand retailer partnerships

Once the business demonstrates value, the company can expand its network of fashion brands and retailers. This increases the range of products available to consumer-platform users and creates more opportunities for affiliate and referral revenue. The company can also offer its retailer service to participating brands, where appropriate.

Phase 4: Develop premium services

Once the company has established a customer base and a meaningful number of retailer relationships, it can explore sponsored placements, premium analytics, enterprise agreements, and additional commercial services. The timing and investment in these services should depend on demonstrated demand.

10. Long-Term Vision

The company's long-term objective is to become an AI-powered fashion discovery and retail technology platform that connects consumers, fashion brands, and online retailers.

For consumers, the platform offers a more convenient way to discover relevant fashion products. For retailers, it provides an additional customer acquisition channel and an AI shopping assistant that can be integrated into their own online stores. For brands, it creates opportunities to reach customers whose shopping preferences match their products.

The business combines commission-based revenue from customer referrals with recurring B2B revenue from retailer subscriptions. Additional services can expand the company's commercial offering as the platform grows.

By serving both consumers and retailers, the company can pursue multiple revenue streams while building a business around the common goal of making fashion shopping more effective.

Final business positioning: An AI-powered fashion discovery and retail technology platform that helps consumers find relevant products while enabling retailers to improve product discovery and generate sales. The business combines a consumer-facing shopping platform with a paid AI shopping assistant for retailers. Revenue comes from affiliate commissions, direct referral agreements, recurring subscriptions, and additional commercial services. Its strategy is to build a scalable business that benefits from both consumer adoption and retailer demand, without requiring the company to own inventory or manage product fulfillment. The financial scenarios illustrate possible revenue outcomes at different levels of adoption. The next step is to validate the underlying assumptions through customer acquisition, retailer partnerships, and paying pilot customers.
