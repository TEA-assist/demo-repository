Haya AI — Future Features & Product Roadmap

Feature Planning Document | October 2026

Overview

This document outlines potential features and future development opportunities for Haya AI.

The objective is to expand Haya from an AI-powered fashion product discovery platform into a comprehensive fashion assistant and shopping ecosystem.

Features are organized into categories for team discussion, evaluation, and roadmap planning. Priorities and implementation timelines will be determined by the management team.

14. Roadmap Planning

Features will be evaluated and assigned to development phases based on management decisions.

The following criteria can be used during roadmap planning:

Evaluation CriteriaDescription
Customer ValueExpected improvement to the shopping experience.
Business ValuePotential contribution to revenue, growth, or retailer acquisition.
Development ComplexityEstimated technical effort and implementation requirements.
Infrastructure CostExpected costs of AI models, APIs, storage, and computing resources.
DependenciesOther features, integrations, or infrastructure required before implementation.
Market DemandCustomer and retailer interest in the feature.
Competitive DifferentiationPotential to distinguish Haya from other AI shopping platforms.
Operational RequirementsOngoing maintenance, customer support, compliance, and external dependencies.

Feature priorities, development phases, ownership, and delivery timelines will be defined following team review.
